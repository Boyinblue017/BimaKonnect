Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how BimaKonnect handles information when you use our insurance education website. BimaKonnect provides general educational information; it does not provide insurance quotes, make coverage decisions, or replace a licensed insurance professional.

2. Information you provide

When you use the assistant, recommendation quiz, glossary, or claims guide, the app receives the question or choices you submit in order to generate your response. The current MVP does not create user accounts or deliberately store chat messages, quiz answers, or claims-guide selections in an application database.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information such as national ID numbers, policy numbers, medical records, bank or mobile-money details, passwords, or another person's personal information.

3. Technical information and hosting

Like most websites, our hosting provider may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and error logs to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. These logs are subject to the hosting provider's own retention and privacy practices. We do not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

4. How we use information

We use submitted information only to operate the requested feature, protect the service, and diagnose technical issues. We do not use it to make automated insurance eligibility, pricing, or claims decisions.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to host and secure BimaKonnect, or where required by law. The application does not retain submitted questions or selections after responding; hosting-provider logs may be retained according to the provider's settings.

6. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit information and can request information about any personal data we hold, or ask for correction, deletion, or to object to processing where applicable. If you are in Kenya, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner describes rights including being informed, access, objection, correction, and deletion. Learn about these rights from the ODPC.

7. Children

BimaKonnect is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you are under the age required to consent to data processing where you live, use the service with a parent or guardian and do not submit personal details.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the service, but no internet transmission or system is completely secure. Avoid submitting sensitive information through this MVP.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as BimaKonnect develops. The date above shows the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact oti3no@proton.me.
